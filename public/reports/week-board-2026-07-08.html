What changed since v1 (your four asks)

  • NewTap a card to move it. Any card — on a day, unslotted, or backlog — opens a sheet: pick a day (Mon–Fri or "no day"), pick a block (🌅/🌆/anytime), tap Move. It updates the task's day and reminder instantly. I chose tap-over-drag because dragging on a scrolling phone screen misfires constantly; tap is reliable. Drag can still come later.
    One honest catch: the app can't touch your Google Calendar, so when YOU move a card, the 🔨 calendar block doesn't move at that instant — the next routine check-in (7am daily) now has a reconcile step that finds every mismatched 🔨 event and moves it to match the board. Task, reminder, and board are instant; the calendar mirror catches up by morning.
  • NewBacklog on the board. Below the week you now see "Backlog — not in this week": every open task that isn't in the focus week. Tap one → "Add to this week" with a day and block. The Quests tab remains the full-management view; the board's backlog is for slotting.
  • NewUnfinished tasks get pressed, not shuffled. Done tasks show grayed with a ✓ (unchanged). But now, when the 7am routine finds yesterday's task still open, it doesn't just quietly roll it — the DM names it, says where it was tentatively re-slotted, and asks you straight: "when will you actually finish this?" Reply in the Claude app to lock a real time. If something slips twice, it says so.
  • NewTimezone travels with you. Blocks and times on the board now display in whatever timezone your phone is in — this was easy, one-line-per-function easy, because a reminder is an absolute moment and the board just renders it locally. Your meetings need nothing: Google Calendar events shift automatically when your phone changes timezone; that's built into how calendars work. The one thing that stays Denver-pinned is which Monday a week belongs to, so the board and the routines never disagree about what "this week" means. The routines also still CREATE reminders anchored to Denver 9am/1pm — making them schedule around your travel location is the genuinely hard part (they'd need to know where you are), so that's not in this build.

Unchanged from v1

  • Weekend shows the week that just ended (next week doesn't exist until Monday 7am); ▸ peeks ahead.
  • Mon–Fri swipe columns opening on today, area colors, priority flags, live updates when a routine or chat moves something.
  • No database changes. 120 automated tests green (24 new for this feature), production build clean.

Quiz — 6 questions (3 changed)

1. You tap "Fix Phones" and move it from Tuesday morning to Wednesday afternoon on the board. What's true immediately, and what's true only after the next 7am check-in?

Check your answer

Immediately: the task's day and reminder change — your phone will now ping Wednesday ~1pm, and the board shows it Wednesday 🌆. By next 7am: the routine notices the 🔨 calendar event still sits on Tuesday and moves it to Wednesday 1–3pm. The calendar mirror lags; the task never does.

2. Where do you find a task that ISN'T part of this week's plan, and how do you get it onto Thursday morning?

Check your answer

Scroll below the week to "Backlog — not in this week." Tap the task → pick Thu → 🌅 Morning → "Add to this week." It joins the focus week with a Thursday 9am reminder.

3. What single thing decides a task's block, and what else does that same thing control?

Check your answer

The reminder time: before noon = 🌅, noon+ = 🌆. It's also when your phone pings — moving blocks moves the ping.

4. Tuesday's task didn't get done. What exactly does Wednesday 7am look like now?

Check your answer

The DM has a "🔁 Unfinished" section: it names the task, says it was planned Tuesday and is still open, says where it was tentatively moved (e.g. today 9am), and asks you directly when you'll actually finish it — you reply in the Claude app to commit to a time. It won't slide by silently, and a second slip gets called out.

5. You're in New York. A task has a 9:00am Denver reminder, and you have a 4pm Denver meeting. What does each show on your phone?

Check your answer

The task shows 11:00a New York time on the board (and pings at that moment) — the board renders in your phone's timezone. The meeting shows 6pm — Google Calendar does that shift natively, nothing we built. Both are the same absolute moment they always were.

6. It's Saturday. Which week does the board show and why — and what stays pinned to Denver even when you travel?

Check your answer

The week that just ended, because next week's plan doesn't exist until Monday 7am (▸ peeks ahead). "Which Monday a week belongs to" stays Denver-pinned so the board and routines always agree on what "this week" means — only the display of times follows your phone.
